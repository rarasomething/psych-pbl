      How to play

      Your snake is a mind under pressure. Survive as long as you can while negative thoughts pile up.

      • Move with the arrow keys or WASD. The snake waits for your first key before it starts.
      • Red squares are cognitive distortions. Eating one grows your trail and speeds the game up. New ones keep appearing whether you eat them or not.
      • Bright diamonds are coping skills. They're rarer, and each pushes back differently: Checking the Evidence shrinks your trail, Reframing the Thought clears distortions off the board, and Taking a Small Step slows the pace for a few seconds.
      • Don't hit the walls or your own trail. Your score is how many seconds you survive.
      • Click the ? next to anything in the key to see what it means. The game pauses while you read.
